## 📊 Risk Analysis

### What Could Go Wrong?

**Risk 1: No one books consultations**
- Mitigation: Price test ($297 vs $497 vs $697)
- Pivot: Maybe market wants free + ads model
- Learning: Validate before building big membership platform

**Risk 2: Membership churn is high**
- Mitigation: Monthly community value (live Q&A, new content)
- Benchmark: Industry avg churn is 5-7%/month, aim for <3%
- Solution: Make community indispensable (connections > content)

**Risk 3: Bundle margins compressed**
- Mitigation: Don't compete on price, compete on curation + support
- Differentiation: Pre-tested, perfectly integrated, 90-day support
- Alternative: If margins drop, pivot to consulting/membership focus

**Risk 4: Platform competition**
- Mitigation: First-mover advantage, build moat with data
- Reality: By year 5, we'll have data no one else has
- Backup: Even without platform, bundles + membership = $1M+

**Risk 5: Takes longer than projected**
- Mitigation: Conservative projections (50% of optimistic)
- Flexibility: Adjust phases based on what's working
- Fallback: Even at half the speed, we hit $500k by year 6
